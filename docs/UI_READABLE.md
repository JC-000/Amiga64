# UI that stays readable after c2v

Wave 15, package W15-U (`docs/WAVE15_BRIEF.md`). These are the rules for an
Amiga program's screen that must read well on the C64 after our
planar→VIC conversion (`docs/C2V.md`, CONTRACTS "Display refresh (S3.5)").
The MOD player's screen (`fixtures/mod/ui/`) follows them, and so can any
later guest UI.

**Status:** U0 ruled (2026-10-12): H3, the rules, the font, the palette, the blit kick and the addresses are approved and binding. U1/U2 are implemented.

## What the conversion does (the facts the rules come from)

- **Lores, 2 planes → multicolour 160 px** (mc2). Each MC pixel is the
  *left* lores pixel of its pair, so a 1-px column can vanish. There are
  4 colours for the whole screen (`c2v_pal_mc2` fills the whole matrix
  with one value). F1 (the 1:1 view) does nothing for lores.
- **Hires, 1-4 planes → hires 320 px** (fit view). Each C64 pixel is the
  OR of a hires pixel *pair*, so 1-px gaps close and 8-px-wide Topaz
  glyphs become 4 px wide. This is the AROS insert-disk text: "visible,
  but too compressed to read". Colours are per 8x8 cell (16 hires px x 8
  lines). The background is pen 0 everywhere. The foreground is the pen
  of the cell's first set pixel (on its first line with one); every other
  set pixel in the cell takes that colour.
- **Hires in the 1:1 view (F1):** one hires pixel is one C64 pixel, in a
  320x200 window that starts at byte `disp_px` (20 = centred). Each cell
  is exact with two pens: the top-left pixel's pen and one other.
- **Colours** go through the OCS→VIC LUT (nearest in CIE L\*a\*b\*). Then
  W12-V's `separate` gives pens with different OCS colours different VIC
  colours, walking the luma ladder when a pen's nearest colour is taken.
- **Fit view, vertically:** the first 200 lines from DIWSTRT (W14-D). Any
  line below 200 is not shown.
- **Refresh:** a conversion happens on a display-field change (`gen`) or a
  pixel-epoch change (`disp_st+105`, bumped by every D-enabled blit).
  It never happens on CPU writes to bitplanes. Inside `mach_run` it waits
  for `DISP_N` = 24 guest frames after the previous conversion ended.

## The display (H3)

**Hires, 640x200, 3 planes, every design pixel two hires pixels wide.**
The design grid is therefore 320x200, the C64's own hires grid. The pair-OR
then loses nothing: the fit view is pixel-exact, at C64 hires resolution,
with 40x25 cells of 8x8.

- BPLCON0 `$B200`; DIWSTRT/STOP `$2C81`/`$F4C1` (200 lines);
  DDFSTRT/STOP `$003C`/`$00D4` (80 bytes).
- The planes are interleaved per line: plane p of line y is at
  `planes + y*240 + p*80`, and BPL1MOD = BPL2MOD = 160. One glyph line in
  every plane is then reachable from one address register by d16.
- 8 pens (palette below). Each cell shows one of pens 1-7 on pen 0.
- The copper list reloads BPL1PT-BPL3PT every frame (6 MOVEs). Everything
  else is set once by `ui_init`.
- On a real Amiga the screen looks the same: a 320-px picture made of
  hires pixel pairs.

**Rejected alternative L2 (lores 2 planes, mc2):** 160 MC pixels across,
so this 8x8 font fits 20 columns (preview `alt_l2_fit.png`), with 4
colours for the whole screen (no VU colours). A 4-px font would give 40
columns, but at 3x7 pixels M, W and N are hard to tell apart. L2's one
advantage is cost: an mc2 conversion is about half a hires one (C2V.md
"Performance": 362,839 vs 675,439 φ2 per 200 lines). See "Cost" for why
that might matter.

## The font

8x8 cells, glyphs 7 pixels wide (columns 0-6) with 3-pixel counters,
square letterforms with chamfered corners, 2-pixel vertical strokes,
column 7 the letter gap, row 7 the line gap (descenders: `, ; _`).
`a`-`z` fold to upper case; `$80-$82` are play, stop and pause.

**Provenance (fix round 1).** The first font (U0, 6 pixels wide) was not
original. 36 of its 63 glyphs were byte-identical to the C64 character ROM
(chargen 901225-01, upper case), and 12 more were within 2 pixels: it was
drawn from memory of the C64's typeface. rev15-ui found it, and the
supervisor ruled a redraw (B1). The new font changes the proportions
(7-pixel glyphs, 3-pixel counters) so that no glyph can fall back onto the
ROM's 6-pixel forms, and rule F4 checks it against the ROM.

## The rules

Each rule has a check in `host/ui_preview.py`. The same functions run on
the guest's real planes and the C64's real output in `tests/run_modui.py`.

| Rule | Statement | Check |
|---|---|---|
| **R1 mode** | Hires, 1-3 planes, no HAM/DPF, DDF 80 bytes: `disp_decide` gives HIRES. | `check_mode` |
| **R2 grid** | Every hires pixel pair is equal (design on the 320 grid). | `check_grid`: no plane byte with `((b >> 1) ^ b) & $55` |
| **R3 exact** | The VIC picture equals the design pixel for pixel, each pen in its VIC colour. The fit view: design x at C64 x. The 1:1 view: hires x = 8·px + x. | `check_exact` (both views): renders the C64 bitmap, matrix and colour RAM as the VIC reads them (`vic_pixels`) |
| **R4 colour** | (a) Each pen in use keeps its intended VIC colour (pass 1 of `separate`, no ladder fallback). (b) Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 against the other colour of its cell (WCAG 2 AA, Pepto sRGB); bars and rules ≥ 3:1. (c) Any two pens in use ≥ 15 CIE76 ΔE apart. | `check_colours` |
| **R5 cells** | Every 8x8 cell holds at most one pen besides pen 0. | `check_cells` (on the planes) |
| **R6 top 200** | DIW ≤ 200 lines, so the fit view crops nothing. | `check_mode` |
| **R7 1:1** | F1 must not break the screen: the 1:1 window is pixel-exact too (a 2x zoom of the centre half). The fit view is the intended view. | `check_exact(view=1)` |
| **F1 glyph box** | Glyphs use rows 0-6; row 7 is the line gap (except `, ; _`). | `check_font` |
| **F2 strokes** | Vertical strokes are 2 px wide: at most 2 pixels per glyph without a horizontal neighbour (M's and W's centre stubs). | `check_font` |
| **F3 distinct** | Every glyph is unchanged by the conversion, and any two glyphs, the space included (a minimum ink), differ in ≥ 4 pixels after it. | `check_font`, `min_distance` (now 4: `5`/`6`, `6`/`G`, `U`/`W`, `:`/`;`) |
| **F4 clean room** | Every glyph is ≥ 8 pixels from the C64 character ROM's glyph for the same character, also under ±1-pixel shifts. Exempt: the space, `-` and `.` (shapes the cell forces; `:` passes at 8). The ROM is read from the local VICE install, never stored. | `run_modui --font-vs-rom` (→ `build/evidence/w15-ui/fix1/font_vs_rom.txt`), `test_ui_iface.test_font_vs_c64_rom` (skipped without the ROM, naming the paths tried) |

### Palette

| Pen | Use | OCS | VIC | Contrast on black |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | background | `$000` | 0 black | — |
| 1 | values | `$FFF` | 1 white | 21.0 |
| 2 | labels | `$999` | 15 light grey | 7.0 |
| 3 | headings, title bar, play marker | `$7AA` | 3 cyan | 7.6 |
| 4 | VU low | `$584` | 5 green | 5.3 |
| 5 | VU mid | `$BC7` | 7 yellow | 11.4 |
| 6 | VU high | `$965` | 10 light red | 4.46 (a shape: needs 3) |
| 7 | rule lines | `$66B` | 14 light blue | 3.9 (shapes only) |

Pens 4-7 are used only for shapes (bars, rules), which need 3:1; text
uses pens 1-3 (and pen 0 on pen 3 in the title bar, 7.6:1).

Each OCS value is the closest OCS colour to its VIC target, so the LUT
maps it home and `separate` never moves it. Light grey and cyan are the
closest pair, at ΔE 18.8. Light green and yellow (16.7) were rejected for
the VU bar.

## Drawing (the kit, U1/U2)

- **A static pen map.** The layout fixes each cell's pen, like colour RAM.
  The kit writes only the planes whose bit is set in that pen; the other
  planes of that cell stay zero from `ui_init`'s clear. A white glyph is
  8 word stores (`pen1`), a grey one 8 (`pen2`), a cyan one 16 (`pen3`).
  The title bar's inverse text is the complement in pen 3's planes.
- **The font** is indexed by the byte itself (256 x 16 bytes, the
  mapping of `glyph_code` baked in), so any byte draws with no lookup.
- **The cycle.** `ui_frame` works in a cycle of `UI_CADENCE` = 12 calls
  (supervisor ruling 3: at most ~6 kicks per second at 72 Hz):

  | Phase | Work |
  |---|---|
  | 0 | the epoch kick, if anything was drawn in the last cycle; then the header (POS, length, PAT, ROW, SPD, BPM, TIME, play/stop, timer, LOOP, name) |
  | 1-4 | channel strip 0-3: instrument, note, volume, effect, VU bar |
  | 5-8 | trail line 0-3 (variant A), refreshed every `TRAIL_EVERY` = 2 cycles |

  Every call also runs the **trail capture**: a (position, row) different
  from the last one captured stores the row's number and each channel's
  note, effect and param in a ring of 8. A field is drawn only when the
  value it shows changed (a shadow per field). The trail formats each
  captured row once (cached in its ring entry) and draws only the
  characters that differ from what the line shows (compared 4 at a time).
- **VU bars** draw only the cells between the old end and the new one.
  Each cell is 6 lines of its pen's planes.
- **Refresh: the kick.** At phase 0, if anything was drawn since the last
  kick, the kit starts a 1-word D-only blit into `UI_KICK`. blt.s then
  bumps the pixel epoch, and disp.s converts again (under its `DISP_N`
  budget).
- **`ui_init` order** (found on VICE): registers, the copper list,
  `COP1LC`, the `COPJMP1` strobe, then DMAEN|BLTEN for the clear blit, and
  BPLEN|COPEN only at the end. With COPEN on before COP1LC is loaded, every
  frame boundary runs the copper from COP1LC = 0, i.e. guest `$000000`. On
  our REU fill (`$FF00,$00FF`) that is "MOVE `$00FF` to BPLCON0" 4000
  times, so BPLCON0 ended `$00FF` (DEPTH, never shown). A real Amiga does
  the same, so this is the program's rule, not a runtime bug.
- **Any value is legal** (I-1): numbers are clamped to their field width,
  bytes below 32 show as spaces, other unknown bytes as `?`, `a`-`z` fold
  to upper case, notes outside 0-35 show `---`, an effect above F shows
  `?` (then the param, e.g. `?44`; fix round 1, was the low nibble), VU
  above 64 shows as 64, the time is capped at 99:59, the name stops at its
  first NUL.
- **Trail variants** (supervisor ruling 6): A (default) is 4 lines at a
  16-line pitch, rows 18/20/22/24. B (`TRAIL_N=7 TRAIL_PITCH=1
  TRAIL_DIM=1`) is 7 lines at an 8-line pitch, with older rows dimmed
  (pen 2). The newest row is always the bottom line, marked by a cyan
  play glyph.

## Memory (ruled: W15-U owns guest chip RAM $040000-$07FFFF)

| Range | Use |
|---|---|
| `$070000-$0700FF` | copper list (BPL1PT-BPL3PT, end) |
| `$070100-$070101` | `UI_KICK`, the epoch blit's target word |
| `$071000-$07CB7F` | planes, 3 x 80 x 200, interleaved (no row crosses $080000) |
| `$040000-$06FFFF`, `$07CB80-$07FFFF` | spare (UI data if needed) |

Others: the program image is at `$010000-$03FFFF` (W15-M) and holds the
kit's code, font and BSS; the MOD is at `$080000`+; `mod_view` is at
`$000400`; params/status are at `$000480-$0004FF`. The kit only reads
`mod_view`, and includes `fixtures/mod/mod_view.h` (W15-M) read-only.

## Budget (ruled)

`ui_frame` runs at 72.08 Hz (s = 0 on the U64E) and is preempted by the
replay's CIA interrupt. Its budget is **an average of ≤ 400 68k
instructions per call, and ≤ 1500 at worst**. The kit meets it by
construction (see "Drawing"): a 12-call cycle with one phase of work per
call, fields drawn only on a change of the value they show, VU cells only
between the old and new end, and the trail refreshed every
`TRAIL_EVERY` = 2 cycles from text formatted once per row. The measured
numbers are under "Cost".

## Cost (measured, VICE, U2)

The per-call counts come from `tests/run_modui.py` leg `kit`: the 68k
instructions of one `ui_frame` call (the runner's `icount`, minus the call
frame).

| Sequence | Variant A (default): average | worst | Variant B: average | worst |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| song: speed 6, 40% note triggers per row (240 calls) | 257 | 917 | 319 | 922 |
| stress: speed 1, every channel triggers every row (120 calls) | 347 | 935 | 444 | 938 |
| every call of the run (504 calls, extreme values included) | | 1216 | | 1216 |

`TRAIL_EVERY` = 1 (the trail every cycle) measured 327 / 482 (song /
stress average) for A. 2 is the default, and the budget holds in every
sequence. B's stress average exceeds 400.

- `ui_init`: 4,905 instructions (A) / 5,277 (B): one full draw, plus
  the clear blit.
- Copper: 6 MOVEs per frame, ~3,300 φ2 (~545 each, CONTRACTS "Copper").
- Conversion stall: a hires conversion is ~675k φ2 (C2V.md), at most once
  per kick (≤ 6/s), and under `DISP_N`. Ruling 3 accepts it: the replay
  counts owed ticks.

- Host: `python3 -m pytest host/tests/test_ui_rules.py` (green on the font
  sample and the player screen; any `mod_view` values; each mutant killed
  on its own rule). Previews: `python3 -m host.ui_preview --out DIR`.
- **Mutants** (`ui_preview.MUTANTS`, ≤ 10). Each must fail:

  | Mutant | What it does | Fails |
  |---|---|---|
  | `thin` | Topaz-style 1-hires-px glyphs, 80 columns | R2 (and R3) |
  | `stroke1` | 1-px strokes on the 320 grid | F2 |
  | `close` | labels `$333` on black | R4b |
  | `close2` | VU-mid `$585` next to VU-low `$584` | R4a |
  | `same` | `O` drawn as `0` | F3 |
  | `lores` | BPLCON0 lores 2 planes | R1 |
  | `tall` | a 256-line DIW | R6 |
  | `cell` | VU colour segments shifted half a cell | R5 |

- VICE (`python3 -m tests.run_modui --prg build/runner.prg [--variant B]`):
  - **kit**, outside `mach_run`, instruction-counted:
    - **Setup.** `t_preset` (DMACON SET AUD0-3EN, INTENA SET PORTS|AUD0:
      what the replay owns), then `ui_init`.
    - **The sequences.** The song (240 calls) and the stress sequence (120
      calls), then three special views: the name with bytes after a NUL,
      BPM 5, 100 and 105, elapsed 300,000-3,276,799, and effects above F.
      Then extreme values.
    - **Every run's custom writes** (the chip log) are checked against the
      UI's allowlist. DMACON may only be SET with DMAEN, BPLEN, COPEN or
      BLTEN; no CIA, Paula ($0A0-$0DF), INTENA or INTREQ write.
    - **Reads are not gated** on VICE: the chip log records writes only.
      `host/tests/test_ui_static.py` covers them statically. It resolves
      ui.s's equates and fails on any operand that addresses a CIA
      (`$BFxxxx`), INTENA/INTREQ or `$DFF0A0-$DFF0DF`. Its mutants include
      rev15-ui's `tst.b $bfdd00`. Limit: an operand computed by an
      expression over equates is not resolved.
    - **Kicks** (`blt_n`) ≤ calls / 12 + 1.
    - **At each checkpoint** (the view held for 3 cycles):
      - the planes equal the model byte for byte;
      - `decode` (the planes back to glyphs) equals `expect_fields` (the
        text written from I-1 alone);
      - the guest's palette and plane pointers are the approved ones;
      - R1-R7 hold on the guest's planes with the guest's own registers
        (`cop_sh`: BPLCON0, DIW, DDF, modulos, palette).
    - **At the end**, DMACONR still has AUD0-3EN and INTENAR is unchanged.
    - **Gates:** average ≤ 400 on the song, worst ≤ 1500 on every call.
  - **live**, under `mach_run` (s = 2), after `t_preset`: a staged script,
    one `ui_frame` per VERTB, then idle calls.
    - The planes equal the model, and the decode holds.
    - The C64's bitmap, matrix, colour RAM and VIC registers equal
      `c2v_ref.disp_convert` of the planes.
    - `disp_mode` is HIRES, and R3 holds on the C64's real pixels.
    - Kicks ≤ calls / 12 + 3.
    - DMACON's AUD bits and INTENA are unchanged.
    - **R7 on VICE:** `disp_view` := 1 at px 20, then `disp_refresh`. The
      C64 must equal `disp_convert_view`.
    - PNGs of both views, read back from VICE.
  - **68k mutants** (`--mutants`, 10). Each must fail the check named
    below. A crash does not count as a kill.

    | Mutant | What it does | Must fail on |
    |---|---|---|
    | `pal` | label pen `$333` | palette |
    | `plane` | pen 2 into plane 0 | plane bytes |
    | `nokick` | no epoch kick | live: bitmap |
    | `font1` | 1-px-stroke font | plane bytes |
    | `iface` | ui_init clears AUD0-3EN and INTEN | interface |
    | `kickall` | a kick on every call | kick rate |
    | `tall` | DIWSTOP `$2CC1` (256 lines) | R6 |
    | `nonul` | the name not cut at its NUL | decode |
    | `bpm0` | BPM tens shown as space after a hundreds digit | decode |
    | `time` | the 99:59 cap at 3,276,800 (divu overflow) | decode |
- Host: `host/tests/test_ui_rules.py` (the rules and host mutants) and
  `host/tests/test_ui_iface.py`:
  - the decode against `expect_fields` on special and random views;
  - `mod_view.h`'s offsets against ui.s's equates;
